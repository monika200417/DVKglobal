Corporate Philosophy

About Us

Established in 2018, DVK Global has evolved from a regional insurance surveying firm into a growing organization with ambitions to serve clients across India and international markets. Guided by a team of highly experienced directors, each possessing over 15 years of expertise in handling small, medium, and high-value claims, we provide comprehensive insurance survey and loss assessment services across Fire, Engineering, Marine Cargo, Motor, and Miscellaneous lines of business.

With the addition of specialized departments and experienced professionals, we are expanding our operations into major cities across India while maintaining our commitment to independence, transparency, technical excellence, and timely service.

Our Mission

To provide independent, transparent, and professional insurance survey and loss assessment services that protect the interests of both insurers and insured. We are committed to delivering accurate, fair, and timely assessments through technical expertise, ethical practices, and industry knowledge.

Our Vision

To become one of India's most trusted Insurance Surveyor and Loss Assessor organizations with a strong Pan India presence and a growing international footprint.

We aspire to create opportunities for young professionals by nurturing technical excellence, ethical leadership, and innovation, while delivering world-class insurance surveying and risk assessment services that contribute to a stronger global insurance ecosystem. Our long-term objective is to become a trusted partner for insurers, brokers, corporates, financial institutions, and insured clients by delivering accurate, unbiased, and professional claim assessment services that meet the highest industry standards.

Services & Scope of Expertise

Core Insurance Departments

🔥 Fire Insurance

Assessment of property damage, stock losses, machinery losses, and business interruption arising from fire and allied perils.

⚙️ Engineering Insurance

Survey and evaluation of machinery breakdown, erection risks, contractor risks, and electronic equipment.

🚢 Marine Cargo

Inspection, supervision, and assessment of transit losses involving imports, exports, and project cargo.

🚗 Motor Insurance

Assessment of private, commercial, heavy vehicle, and specialized motor claims.

📋 Miscellaneous Insurance

Evaluation of liability, burglary, money, fidelity guarantee, and other miscellaneous claims.

Specialized Services

Risk Inspections

Identifying vulnerabilities and recommending preventive actions.

Predispatch

Ensuring quality and compliance before cargo dispatch.

Cargo Supervision

Monitoring handling and movement of cargo to minimize risk.
