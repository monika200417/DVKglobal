Insurance Survey & Loss Assessment

Five core disciplines. Three specialized services.

DVK Global conducts professional survey and loss assessment across all major insurance lines, backed by IRDAI licensed surveyors, chartered engineers, chartered accountants and domain specialists.

Core Insurance Lines

Fire Insurance

Assessment of property damage, stock losses, machinery losses and business interruption arising from fire and allied perils.

Engineering Insurance

Survey and evaluation of machinery breakdown, erection all risks, contractor's all risks, electronic equipment and industrial claims.

Marine Cargo

Inspection, supervision and assessment of transit losses involving imports, exports, inland transit and project cargo movements.

Motor Insurance

Assessment of private, commercial, heavy vehicle and specialized motor claims with prompt on-site inspection and reporting.

Miscellaneous Insurance

Evaluation of liability, burglary, money, fidelity guarantee and other miscellaneous insurance claims with professional accuracy.

Specialized Services

Risk Inspections

Identifying vulnerabilities and recommending preventive measures to help insurers and insured minimize potential loss exposure.

Pre-Dispatch Inspections

Ensuring quality and compliance before goods are dispatched — reducing transit risk and supporting insurance documentation.

Cargo Supervisions

Monitoring handling and movement of cargo to minimize risk during loading, transit and unloading operations.
